        How the plan works

        Each day, your lineup starts your best healthy players with a game, by expected points, up to 6 F, 4 D and 2 G. A move only scores when the new player fills a slot that would otherwise sit empty or beats someone already in it.

        The planner checks every combination of who sits in each stream spot day by day, within your remaining adds, and keeps the best one. It won't re-add a player it dropped earlier in the week, since dropped players go to waivers.

        It assumes an add can play the same day. Make moves before that day's first puck drop; if your league holds new adds until the next day, make each move a day early. Goalie value is weighted by each goalie's projected share of starts, so check confirmed starters before adding one. Weeks run Monday to Sunday; if Yahoo merged the short opening week with the next one, plan the two together.

        How this is built

        Projections and scoring come from your two rankings files. Anyone on the six rosters you pasted counts as rostered; everyone else counts as a free agent. IR players count as rostered but are left out of lineups.

        Free agents need at least projected games to appear, so call-ups who barely play don't skew the curve. Goalie points per game are per start.

        Six teams leave a deep waiver pool, and the other five managers stream too. That makes rank 3 a more realistic replacement level than rank 1. The games-per-week setting is a rough stand-in for streaming; the Weekly streaming tab uses the real schedule and your 4-add limit instead.
